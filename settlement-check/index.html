Card settlement check, built in n8n

Did every card sale reach the bank? This demo checks, and it stops with an alert when it can't get reliable bank data.

All data is made up and the bank feeds are simulated. I built this demo with AI help. The video is narrated with an AI version of my voice, and AI made the screen recording.

What the normal run finds

  • 10 card batches: 6 matched, 1 still inside the 4-day window and 3 that need review.
  • The other 2 review items are bank lines: a duplicate deposit and one that can't be identified.
  • Every bank line is accounted for once, or the run stops.

Limits

  • It isn't connected to a real POS, bank or QuickBooks.
  • It uses one flat card fee. Real processors vary by card type.
  • The alert is a file until Telegram or email is connected.
